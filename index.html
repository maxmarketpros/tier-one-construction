Meet Tier-One

Early Decisions Shape the Whole Project

Tom Gill began in the building trades at age 12 and brings 65 years of field and project-management experience to Tier-One. His guiding principle is simple: “The first 30 days determine a project's outcome.”

The training is built around practical questions: What is known? What is still assumed? Who needs to decide? How will the team know when the plan has changed? The answers matter in preconstruction, estimating, scheduling, contract work, and day-to-day project management.

Tier-One Approach

Training Tied to Real Construction Work

The goal is to make planning and project controls useful in the next meeting and on the next job.

  • Start With the First 30 Days

    Identify the decisions and handoffs that shape the rest of the project.

  • Use Project Scenarios

    Connect concepts to federal, commercial, and industrial construction situations.

  • Make Assumptions Visible

    Record what is known, unknown, and awaiting direction.

  • Keep Teams Aligned

    Give field and office staff a shared way to discuss changes.

Construction Training Questions in Tampa Bay

Browse All FAQs
What kinds of construction teams can request training?

Tier-One offers training for federal, commercial, and industrial construction work. The best starting point is to describe your team's role, project type, and the issue you want to improve. The session can then be discussed around that need.

Where is Tier-One's primary service area?

The primary area is Tampa Bay, Florida and an approximate 50-mile radius. Thomas sometimes goes to Washington, DC for work, but that travel is occasional and should be confirmed for a specific request.

Can we discuss a workshop before choosing a topic?

Yes. Use the contact form, call, or email with the audience and project questions. That conversation can help identify whether preconstruction, management, estimating, scheduling, risk, or contracting training is the most useful focus.
